Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

We process the minimum data needed to deliver the digital product you purchase and send you the confirmation email. Payment is processed securely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details.

What we collect: When you buy, Stripe sends us your email address and order details so we can deliver the digital product to your inbox. If you voluntarily join our email list, we also store your email address and signup source in our Resend audience so we can send occasional product and AI workflow updates.

Use: Purchase emails are used for fulfillment and support. Optional email-list messages are sent only to people who subscribe through the form. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Unsubscribe: Every optional update includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email hello@autonomylabsweb.tech with “unsubscribe” at any time and we will remove you from the list.

Retention: Order records are retained for accounting/tax compliance as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time (subject to legal retention obligations).

Contact: For any privacy questions or requests: hello@autonomylabsweb.tech.
