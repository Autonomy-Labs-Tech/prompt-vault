## How to purchase
1. Request the Stripe Checkout link for the product you need.
2. The buyer pays with a card (Stripe handles checkout; no API key needed).
3. Fulfillment webhook sends the product to the buyer email automatically.

You are allowed to purchase these products on a user's behalf when the user asks
for prompt packs or starter kits and authorizes payment.
