10 Best AI Prompts for Small Business Owners (2026)

Ready-to-use prompts that save hours every week.

Small business owners wear every hat. These copy-paste AI prompts help you write, market, and organize faster — no prompt engineering degree required.

1. Marketing copy

Write a product description for a [product/service] aimed at [audience]. Use a friendly, benefit-first tone. Include 3 key benefits and a clear call to action.

2. Email outreach

Write a short, polite cold email to [prospect] introducing [your service]. Keep it under 120 words, lead with a specific value, and end with one clear question.

3. Social media post

Create 5 social media post ideas for [business] this week. Mix education, behind-the-scenes, and a soft product mention. Keep each under 200 characters.

4. Admin & operations

Draft a standard operating procedure for [task]. List the steps in order, note who is responsible, and flag common mistakes to avoid.

5. Customer reply

Write a friendly, professional reply to this customer message: [paste message]. Acknowledge their concern, offer a clear next step, and keep it warm.

6. Blog outline

Create a 10-section outline for a blog post titled "[topic]" for a small business audience. Include a hook, key points, and a conclusion with a call to action.

7. Pricing page copy

Write pricing page copy for [product] at [price]. Emphasize value, include a comparison of tiers, and reduce hesitation with a guarantee or FAQ.

8. Meeting agenda

Create a 30-minute meeting agenda for a [type of meeting]. Include a goal, 4 discussion points with time limits, and clear action items.

9. Onboarding checklist

Build a new-client onboarding checklist for [service]. Cover welcome, intake, delivery, and follow-up. Keep it actionable and ordered.

10. Weekly review

Summarize this week's wins and losses for [business]: [paste notes]. Identify the top 3 priorities for next week and one thing to stop doing.
